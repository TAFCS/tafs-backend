\section{Overview}
TAFS exposes two HTTPS APIs to UBL for collecting school fees:
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
  \item \textbf{Bill Inquiry} (\texttt{GET}): returns the student's bill and the exact amount to collect.
  \item \textbf{Bill Payment} (\texttt{POST}): posts a collected payment against that bill.
\end{itemize}
Both APIs take a single identifier, the \texttt{consumer\_number}. Its \textbf{length} decides which amount
is returned (Section~\ref{sec:consumer}).

% ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
\section{Consumer Number}\label{sec:consumer}

\begin{center}
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{0.2\textwidth} >{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{0.25\textwidth} L@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Length} & \textbf{Format} & \textbf{Amount returned} \\
\midrule
5 digits & CC number, zero-padded (\texttt{08088}) & \textbf{Normal amount}: the voucher amount, with no addition. \\
15 digits & 10-digit TAFS 1Bill prefix + 5-digit CC (\texttt{100000000008088}) & \textbf{1Bill amount}: the voucher amount + the 1Bill charge (Section~\ref{sec:onebill}). \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}

\begin{itemize}[nosep]
  \item A CC may be sent with or without leading zeros (\texttt{8088} = \texttt{08088}); any number of up to 5 digits is
        treated as a CC.
  \item A 15-digit number must start with the TAFS 1Bill prefix; its last 5 digits are the CC.
  \item Any other length, or a 15-digit number with a different prefix, returns response code \texttt{01}.
  \item Examples in this document use the prefix \texttt{1000000000}.
\end{itemize}

\section{Amounts}\label{sec:amounts}

\subsection{Voucher amount}
\begin{center}
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}l L@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Payment date} & \textbf{Voucher amount} \\
\midrule
On or before the due date & \texttt{amount\_within\_due\_date} \\

Dates are evaluated in Pakistan Standard Time (UTC+05:00).

\newpage
\subsection{1Bill charge}\label{sec:onebill}
For a 15-digit consumer number, the amount returned is the voucher amount plus the \textbf{1Bill charge}, which the
payer bears. The 1Bill charge is the sum of the 1LINK Same Day Settlement Service Fee and the 1LINK Direct Biller /
Bill Aggregator charge for the transaction slab, \textbf{rounded up to the next whole rupee}:

\begin{center}
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{0.36\textwidth} R R R@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Transaction slab (PKR)} & \textbf{Same Day Settlement} & \textbf{Biller Charge} & \textbf{1Bill Charge} \\
\midrule
Up to 5,000                & 4.00   & 12.50  & \textbf{17} \\
5,001 to 10,000            & 5.00   & 15.50  & \textbf{21} \\
10,001 to 100,000          & 14.00  & 39.00  & \textbf{53} \\
100,001 to 250,000         & 22.00  & 62.50  & \textbf{85} \\
250,001 to 1,000,000       & 44.00  & 125.00 & \textbf{169} \\
1,000,001 to 2,500,000     & 88.00  & 250.00 & \textbf{338} \\
2,500,001 to 5,000,000     & 131.00 & 375.00 & \textbf{506} \\
Above 5,000,000            & 175.00 & 500.00 & \textbf{675} \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}

\noindent\textbf{Slab selection.} The slab is that of the \textbf{transaction amount}, i.e.\ the voucher amount plus
the 1Bill charge. Where adding the charge moves the total into the next slab, the next slab's charge applies.
The within-due-date and after-due-date amounts are each calculated separately.

\begin{center}
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}>{\raggedleft\arraybackslash}p{0.15\textwidth} >{\raggedleft\arraybackslash}p{0.13\textwidth} >{\raggedleft\arraybackslash}p{0.15\textwidth} L@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Voucher amount} & \textbf{1Bill charge} & \textbf{1Bill amount} & \textbf{Note} \\
\midrule
90,000 & 53 & 90,053 & Slab 10,001 to 100,000 \\
91,000 & 53 & 91,053 & Same voucher, after the due date \\
99,980 & 85 & 100,065 & 99,980 + 53 exceeds 100,000, so the next slab applies \\
4,990 & 21 & 5,011 & 4,990 + 17 exceeds 5,000, so the next slab applies \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}

\subsection{Collection rules}
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
  \item The inquiry returns \texttt{amount\_payable}: the exact amount to collect today for the consumer number sent.
  \item The payment must carry exactly that amount. Partial payments and overpayments are rejected (code \texttt{06}).
  \item On a 1Bill payment TAFS credits only the voucher amount to the student; the 1Bill charge is recorded against the
        payment and returned as \texttt{channel\_fee}.
\end{itemize}

\newpage
\section{Voucher Resolution}\label{sec:latest}
Every inquiry and payment resolves to the student's \textbf{most recent voucher}, excluding vouchers cancelled
because a newer voucher replaced them, and vouchers not yet released to the parent.
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
  \item If the student has more than one unpaid voucher, the most recent one is returned and collected first. The older
        one is returned only after the most recent one is paid.
  \item If the student has no payable voucher, the API returns the state of the most recent one (code \texttt{03},
        \texttt{04} or \texttt{05}).
  \item Every voucher includes all arrears owed by the student.
\end{itemize}

\section{Connectivity \& Authentication}

\subsection{Base URL}\label{sec:baseurl}
\begin{lstlisting}
https://api.tafs.cloud/api/v1/ubl
\end{lstlisting}
A separate UAT base URL and test students are provided by TAFS for testing.

\subsection{Headers}
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}lll@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Header} & \textbf{Type} & \textbf{Description} \\
\midrule
\texttt{X-UBL-User} & string & Service user ID issued to UBL \\
\texttt{X-UBL-Password} & string & Password for that service user \\
\texttt{Content-Type} & string & \texttt{application/json} (payment requests) \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
  \item Credentials are issued by TAFS separately and are never sent in the URL.
  \item All traffic is HTTPS (TLS 1.2 or higher). Access can be restricted to UBL's outbound IP addresses.
  \item Missing or wrong credentials return response code \texttt{10}.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{HTTP status}
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
  \item A well-formed, authenticated request always returns \textbf{HTTP 200}. The outcome is in \texttt{response\_code}.
  \item A malformed request, such as a missing field or a non-numeric consumer number, returns \textbf{HTTP 400} with a
        description of the invalid field.
\end{itemize}

\section{API 2 \,--\, Bill Payment}\label{sec:payment}

\begin{lstlisting}
POST /api/v1/ubl/bill-payment
Content-Type: application/json
\end{lstlisting}

\subsection{Request body}
\begin{longtable}{@{}p{0.27\textwidth} p{0.1\textwidth} >{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{0.14\textwidth} >{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{0.4\textwidth}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Field} & \textbf{Type} & \textbf{Required} & \textbf{Description} \\
\midrule
\endhead
\texttt{consumer\_number} & digits & Yes & Same value used in the inquiry \\
\texttt{amount} & number & Yes & Amount collected, whole PKR. Must equal \texttt{amount\_payable} \\
\texttt{channel} & enum & CC only & For a CC: \texttt{OTC}, \texttt{APP} or \texttt{INTERNET\_BANKING}. For a 15-digit number it may be omitted; it is always recorded as \texttt{ONEBILL} \\
\texttt{payment\_method} & enum & Yes & See Section~\ref{sec:method} \\
\texttt{transaction\_id} & string & Yes & UBL's unique transaction reference (max 100 characters) \\
\texttt{transaction\_datetime} & ISO 8601 & Yes & With offset, e.g.\ \texttt{2026-10-05T11:00:00+05:00} \\
\texttt{voucher\_number} & string & Advised & From the inquiry; rejects the payment (code \texttt{07}) if a newer voucher was issued since \\
\texttt{branch\_code} & string & OTC & UBL branch code \\
\texttt{payer\_bank\_code} & string & 1Bill & Bank or wallet the payer paid from \\
\texttt{payer\_bank\_name} & string & 1Bill & Name of that bank or wallet \\
\texttt{instrument\_number} & string & Cheque / PO & Required when \texttt{payment\_method} is \texttt{CHEQUE} or \texttt{PAY\_ORDER} \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

\subsection{Duplicates, retries and timeouts}
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
  \item Posting is idempotent on \texttt{transaction\_id}. On a timeout, resend the \textbf{same} request with the
        \textbf{same} \texttt{transaction\_id}.
  \item Code \texttt{08}: the payment is already recorded; treat as success. It is never booked twice.
  \item Code \texttt{99}: nothing was booked; retry.
\end{itemize}

\section{Response Codes}
\begin{center}
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}l L l@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Code} & \textbf{Meaning} & \textbf{Used by} \\
\midrule
\texttt{00} & Success & Inquiry, Payment \\
\texttt{01} & Consumer number not found or invalid length & Inquiry, Payment \\
\texttt{02} & No voucher has been issued for this student & Inquiry, Payment \\
\texttt{03} & Voucher is already paid & Inquiry, Payment \\
\texttt{04} & Voucher validity has expired & Inquiry, Payment \\
\texttt{05} & Voucher is not payable (written off) & Inquiry, Payment \\
\texttt{06} & Amount does not match the payable amount & Payment \\
\texttt{07} & A newer voucher has been issued; inquire again & Payment \\
\texttt{08} & Duplicate transaction; already posted (treat as success) & Payment \\
\texttt{09} & Invalid request (e.g.\ cheque without \texttt{instrument\_number}, or a CC payment without a \texttt{channel}) & Payment \\
\texttt{10} & Invalid credentials & Inquiry, Payment \\
\texttt{99} & General error; nothing booked, retry later & Inquiry, Payment \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}
